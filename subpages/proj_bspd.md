**Learnings:**
- Circuit Design (how to adapt an existing circuit to do something new)
- PCB Layout

**Project Summary:** In this project, I learned how to design circuits and circuit boards using KiCad. I added a delay circuit and updated the PCB connection to be PCIe instead of M.2. This was my first time interacting with circuit boards and designing them so a few mistakes were made with regards to EMI. The traces were all clumped up in the center area between the different sections and that caused a bit of interference between different signals during testing and utilization of the car.

I also learned the PCB design process where you make your initial design and then make several iterations, each considering various trade offs on where signals are routed.
